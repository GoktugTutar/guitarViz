  WELCOME TO PERDE
Your fretboard, explained.
Explore notes, connect chords and hear what you see. Choose a tab to get started.

    
01 · Explore
Pick a root note and a scale or chord. Yellow dots mark the root. Use Layer to compare a scale and chord: two-color dots belong to both.

    
02 · Progressions
Choose a key and a chord progression. Tap a chord to see its notes, role and position. Set the tempo and press Play progression to hear four beats per chord. Turn on Loop to repeat.

    
03 · Chord Finder
Choose any root and one of eight chord types. Switch between All note locations and Playable positions. Use the arrows or position buttons to explore different fingerings.

    
04 · TAB Editor
Choose a chord and position, or enter your own frets. Pick a whole, half, quarter, eighth or sixteenth note and place it at a bar and beat. Click an event to edit or move it. Add rests, undo changes, play at your tempo or download the TAB. Your score saves in this browser.

    
Read the fretboard
  • Notes: letter names such as G, A and B.
  • Intervals: degrees relative to the root. In G major, G = R (1), A = 2 and B = 3.
  • TAB: fret numbers. 0 means an open string; × means mute.
  • Fingers: available for playable positions. 1 = index, 2 = middle, 3 = ring, 4 = pinky. A connecting bar shows a barre.

    
The high string is on top, just like TAB. On a phone, swipe the fretboard sideways. Tap a note to hear it, or use the play button to hear a scale or chord. The speaker button controls sound when tapping notes.

    
Try standard, Drop D or half-step-down tuning, and 12, 15 or 24 frets. Explorer settings are saved in this browser.
♯ = sharp (one semitone higher) · ♭ = flat (one semitone lower)
